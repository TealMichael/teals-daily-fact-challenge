STEP 2 — IMPORTANT NOTE ABOUT EXISTING STUDENT PINs
Older student accounts stored only one-way PIN hashes. Those original PINs cannot be recovered.
After this update, Teacher -> Classes & Students will show "Reset once" for any such older account and offer:
   Generate visible PINs for older accounts
This creates replacement PINs, updates student login, and keeps the new PIN visible in teacher-only views from then on.

New students created after v2.1 automatically have a visible classroom PIN.

STEP 5 — QUICK CLASSROOM PILOT CHECK
With one test student, complete:

   Daily 10
       -> Fix Your Misses (if any)
       -> Your Focus Practice (8 retrievals)
       -> ⭐ Day Complete

Confirm:
- no placement test appears;
- the Daily timer runs quietly rather than displaying elapsed seconds;
- accuracy ranks before time;
- a missed fact is taught with an array + relationship strategy and must be retried correctly;
- early Focus Practice for an unknown profile leans on 2s/5s/10s anchor relationships;
- Focus Practice stays short and spaced rather than drilling one fact repeatedly;
- student mastery grows only from independent retrieval evidence;
- the teacher-only roster/progress views show PINs beside nicknames;
- student-visible Top 10 does NOT show PINs.

RESEARCH-INFORMED DESIGN IN v2.1
The app uses one-at-a-time active retrieval, conceptual arrays/derived-fact strategies after errors, corrective retry, spaced mixed practice, short daily sessions, and quiet timing. The adaptive profile grows gradually from Daily + Focus evidence instead of a beginning placement test.
