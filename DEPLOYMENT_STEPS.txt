WEEKLY MYSTERY BEHAVIOR
- The reward appears only after: Daily 10 -> Fix Your Misses -> Focus Practice -> Day Complete.
- Monday-Thursday: each completed routine earns the next clue in order.
- Students get exactly ONE guess for the entire week.
- They can use it early or save it.
- Friday: completing the routine shows the full four-clue set, gives a saved guess one final chance, then reveals the answer.
- The mystery is the same across every class.
- It does NOT affect Daily rank, mastery, Stars, or Learning Streaks.
- Students see only their own guess/solve status.

TEACHER DASHBOARD
A new Weekly Mystery tab shows:
- this week's answer and all four clues;
- how many students have unlocked a clue;
- how many guesses have been used;
- how many students solved it;
- a Pick Another Mystery button before the first student earns a clue.

The mystery locks automatically after the first clue is earned, preventing midweek answer changes.
The built-in bank contains 80 curated mysteries across eight categories and does not require live internet access.
