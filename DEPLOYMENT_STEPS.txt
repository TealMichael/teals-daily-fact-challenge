WHAT CHANGED
- Late raffle drawing: a raffle opens on that raffle week's Friday and remains drawable later.
- Prior-week recovery: the teacher sees Last Week's Prize Raffles only when an eligible class still needs a valid saved winner.
- Dashboard priority: Today and Warm-Up come first.
- Daily 10 Setup moved under Classes & Rosters as an occasional class tool.
- Log out is less visually dominant in the dashboard header.

WHAT DID NOT CHANGE
- Multiplication Daily generator and TDFC-DAILY-v1 challenge version.
- Daily 10 scoring, alternate modes, Top 10, mastery isolation, or Fact Fluency behavior.
- Warm-Up/Igniter grading or storage.
- Student login/PIN/persistent-login behavior.
- Weekly Mystery student clues/guesses or raffle eligibility rules.
- AWTRIX scripts or Block 1–3 clock mapping.
- Supabase schema.
