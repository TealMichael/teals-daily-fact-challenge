WHAT CHANGED
Teacher -> Student Tools now includes:
- Bulk move: select several students and move them from one class to another at once.
- Individual move: move one student to a different class.
- Permanent delete: remove a truly accidental/duplicate account only after confirmation.

Moving a student preserves the account, classroom PIN, mastery, Stars, streak, saved Daily work, Focus settings, and Mystery history. Use Move for students entered into the wrong block.

Permanent Delete removes the student account and student-linked learning history. Use it only for duplicates or entries that should not exist.
