WHAT CHANGED
- Student question images on Igniter and Quiz of the Week now display at 460 px instead of 620 px.
- Quiz Questions 2-5 have a Back button.
- Questions 1-4 use Save & Next; Question 5 uses Submit Quiz.
- Previously saved answers refill when a student returns to a question.
- Saving a revised answer updates that same quiz/question record rather than duplicating or ignoring it.
- Final quiz submission still locks the student flow and continues to Daily 10, then the normal Friday routine/Mystery.
